About the fest

Every band of Connexion 26 gets its own stage.

Connexion 26 is Lucafama's signature cultural forum — three days where the whole campus turns into five color-coded zones, each hosting a different kind of talent. No single genre owns the fest; performers, coders, filmmakers, and gamers all get equal light.

Open to students from every college. Solo and team formats across all seventeen events, judged by faculty and industry guests, with same-day results for most categories.
